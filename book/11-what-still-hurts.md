# Chapter 11 — What Still Hurts?

Solution work must periodically stop. An implementation is evidence that something was built, not evidence that economically meaningful work disappeared. Chapter 11 therefore adds no capability: it synthesizes the deterministic evidence from Chapters 3–10 and measures the residual against Chapter 0's `$111,020.00` modeled baseline.

```text
ORIGINAL BURDEN
        ↓
CONFIGURATION
NATIVE INTEGRATION
AUTOMATION
BI
PROCESS CHANGE
        ↓
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ WHAT HAPPENED TO EACH BURDEN?│
└──────────────────────────────┘
        ↓
ELIMINATED
REDUCED

MOVED TO ADMINISTRATION
NEW SUPPORT OBLIGATION
UNKNOWN
        ↓
WHAT STILL HURTS?
```

Run the executable assessment:

```bash

```

## Original burden and residual condition

The structured inventory reconciles sales/report consolidation, identity, e-commerce, purchasing, returns, transfers, inventory investigation, spreadsheet assembly, coordination, accounting, and other exceptions exactly to the original baseline. Each record links Chapter 2 questions, intervention chapters, evidence, status, remaining operational burden, administration, support, uncertainty, and custom-software relevance.

**ELIMINATED** means the tested mechanism disappeared in the fixture; it is not a claim that it can never recur. **REDUCED** means recurring work remains. **UNCHANGED** preserves a mechanism interventions did not materially affect. **UNKNOWN** refuses to turn absent evidence into optimism. Visibility is not elimination: BI can expose an inventory or accounting exception without resolving it.

## Observation is not economic measurement

Before/after counts and step ratios are **OBSERVED LAB RESULT** evidence. Original allocations, administration dollars, and remaining dollars are **MODELED ASSUMPTION** evidence. Where credible, the model applies:

```text
remaining ratio = 1 - observed synthetic reduction ratio
modeled remaining burden = original modeled burden × remaining ratio
```

That proportional conversion is an extrapolation, not a retailer measurement. It is not used blindly: accounting remains unchanged because accounting-side evidence is absent, while acquired-store fragmentation remains unknown.

> The lab observed synthetic reductions in specific reconciliation and reporting mechanisms. The dollar-valued residual burden remains a modeled extrapolation, not measured customer savings.

The resulting modeled burden reduction is also kept separate from Chapter 0's `$51,513.80` original recoverable value. Recoverability and burden are different economic concepts.

## Work can move or appear

Configuration can replace operational labor with identity mappings, report definitions, store groups, connector settings, and alert ownership. Those costs are shown as **MOVED TO ADMINISTRATION**, separately from residual operational burden. Automation also creates retry, validation, monitoring, and failure-handling responsibilities. The fixture marks that **NEW SUPPORT OBLIGATION** but deliberately does not price the complete support surface; that belongs to Chapter 12.

The summary therefore presents:

```text
ORIGINAL OPERATIONAL BURDEN
        ↓
RESIDUAL OPERATIONAL BURDEN
        +
NEW ADMINISTRATION
        +
UNPRICED SUPPORT OBLIGATIONS
```

## What remains material?

The ranking is transparent: remaining categories are ordered by modeled remaining burden, then stable category ID—not by a weighted opportunity score. Accounting reconciliation is an unchanged **TECHNICAL GAP** because its required source evidence is missing. Inventory, purchasing, return, and transfer exceptions are largely **PROCESS RESIDUALS** because visibility or deterministic matching cannot correct physical and governance failures. Mapping upkeep and coordination are **ADMINISTRATION RESIDUALS**. Automation failure handling is a **SUPPORT OBLIGATION**. Acquired-store fragmentation is **UNKNOWN**.

Question residual classifications are kept alongside, rather than replacing, Chapter 2 coverage. A question can be answered while its exceptions remain expensive. Likewise, a report becoming visible is not proof that the underlying burden is economically resolved.

## The lesson

```text
WE IMPLEMENTED SOMETHING
        ↓
MEASURE THE RESIDUAL
        ↓
VALUE ONLY EXISTS IF A MEANINGFUL BURDEN ACTUALLY CHANGED
```

This first residual-economic picture informs the original BUY / CONFIGURE hypothesis but does not decide it. Support economics remain untested, no custom software is recommended, and the current lab verdict remains **UNTESTED**.
